# Why Diffeology?

**Author:** Patrick Iglesias-Zemmour  
**Date:** 2025 (Preprint)  
**Status:** Manifesto / Survey Paper. Will appear in tha "Arnold Journal of Mathematics".
**Keywords:** Diffeology, Singular Spaces, Quotient Spaces, Orbifolds, Symplectic Geometry, Moment Map, Geometric Quantization, Prequantum Groupoid, Diffeological Bundles, Klein Stratification, Noncommutative Geometry.

## 📝 Abstract

Diffeology extends differential geometry to spaces beyond smooth manifolds. This paper explores diffeology's key features and illustrates its utility with examples including singular and quotient spaces, and applications in symplectic geometry and prequantization. Diffeology provides a natural and effective framework for handling complexities from singularities and infinite-dimensional settings.

## Significance: A Manifesto for the Smooth World

This paper serves as both an introduction and a philosophical manifesto for the theory. It argues that Diffeology is not merely a "generalization" of differential geometry, but a **refoundation** of it.

Key arguments include:
1.  **The Kleinian Shift:** It redefines Differential Geometry as the study of the pair $(\mathcal{X}, \text{Diff}(\mathcal{X}))$, placing the group of symmetries at the center, rather than the local chart.
2.  **The Universal Principle:** It presents the "creation myth" of the theory: every diffeological space, no matter how singular, is a quotient of a sum of Euclidean domains. This unifies the source of all smoothness.
3.  **Operational Power:** It demonstrates how this framework solves concrete problems that classical geometry cannot, such as the **geodesics of the torus** (which form a non-manifold space mixing circles and irrational tori) and the **singular symplectic reduction** of infinite-dimensional spaces.
