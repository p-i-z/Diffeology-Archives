# Lectures on Diffeology

- **Author:** Patrick Iglesias-Zemmour
- **Status:** Revised Edition (2026)
- **Publisher:** Beijing World Publishing Corp. (2025)

## 📖 About This Book

This book is a companion volume to the author's main textbook, *Diffeology*. It is based on a series of lectures given at Shantou University in 2020/21 and consists of two parts:

1.  **Lectures (L1-L11):** A structured, example-driven tour through the core concepts and applications of diffeology, from non-commutative geometry to symplectic structures on spaces of paths.
2.  **Blog Notes (B1-B37):** A series of shorter, informal notes and exercises exploring specific topics like the Boman Paradox, the geometry of half-lines, and the diffeomorphisms of the square.

This revised edition includes significant new material, most notably a detailed exposition of the **Boman Paradox** and a refined philosophical perspective on the Kleinian program in geometry.

## 🚀 Key Topics

*   Diffeology and Non-Commutative Geometry (Quasifolds, $C^*$-algebras)
*   The Wire Plane and the Boman Paradox
*   Symplectic and Parasymplectic Diffeology
*   The Prequantum Groupoid and Geometric Quantization by Paths
*   Klein Stratification of Diffeological Spaces
*   The Diffeomorphism Groups of the Square and the Wire Plane

## 🛠️ How to Compile This Book

This book is designed to be compiled with a standard LaTeX engine.

1.  **Prerequisites:** A full LaTeX distribution (e.g., MacTeX 2025 or later).
2.  **Method:** The root source file is `Source/LOD.tex`. You can compile it with `pdflatex` (run it two to three times to resolve references) or using your preferred LaTeX editor.
3.  **Output:** The compilation will produce a PDF of the complete book.
