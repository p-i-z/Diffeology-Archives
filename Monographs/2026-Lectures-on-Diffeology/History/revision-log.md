# Revision Log: Lectures on Diffeology

## Revised Edition (2026)

This log documents the significant changes made between the original 2025 edition and the revised 2026 edition.

### Major Additions

#### New Chapters

| ID | Title | Topic |
|----|-------|-------|
| L11 | Geometric Quantization by Paths | Reinterprets geometric quantization as a quotient of the space of paths within the diffeology framework. |
| B26 | The Wire Plane and its Topology | Introduction of the Wire Plane $\cW$; proof that its D-topology equals Euclidean topology while its dimension is 1 with **Smooth Curves Through Convergent Sequences** — Detailed technical construction |
| B27 | The Diffeomorphism Group of the Wire Plane | Proof that $\Diff(\cW) = \Diff(\RR^2)$ as sets (Boman's theorem), but distinct as diffeological groups |

### Philosophical Updates

- **Preface (Revised Edition):** Added a refined discussion of the Kleinian--Souriau program in light of the Boman Paradox.
- **Preface (Revised Edition):** Added the literary piece "A Wonderland of Smoothness" to capture the spirit of diffeology.
- **Preface (Original):** Added a paragraph ("A refinement of Souriau's radical stance") acknowledging the limits of the "group is the geometry" thesis.

### Corrections and Improvements

- **Notation:** Resolved ambiguities (e.g., distinguishing points in $\RR^2$ from diffeological spaces in B36).
- **Macros:** Standardized macro usage throughout all chapters (`\Val` for set of values, `\Tr` for translation map).
- **Typos:** Numerous typographical and grammatical corrections across all chapters.
- **Formatting:** Unified to "ventilated prose" style (line breaks after punctuation) for better version control.
- **Cross-references:** Fixed broken references and added missing labels.

### Technical Changes to Source Structure

- **Macro files split:**
  - `LOD-dflg-macros.tex` — semantic, content-oriented macros (mathematics)
  - `LOD-display-macros.tex` — presentation-oriented macros (layout, fonts, environments)
- **Font configuration:** Appendix font (Charter) now isolated to the Appendix file, resolving global font conflicts.

### Known Issues (None)

All known issues from the first edition have been addressed. No outstanding errata remain.
