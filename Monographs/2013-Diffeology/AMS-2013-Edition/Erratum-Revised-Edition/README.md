# Diffeology: The Revised Edition (Beijing WPC)

**Status:** Extended Errata & Canonical Reference
**Date:** 2022

## Context
This directory contains the **Revised Edition** of the monograph *Diffeology*, published by Beijing World Publishing Corporation.

## Why this file is here
The original edition was published by the American Mathematical Society (AMS) in 2013 (Mathematical Surveys and Monographs, Vol. 185). Following the publication, several errors—including the definition of groupoid morphisms inherited from the 1985 Thesis—were identified.

Due to contractual limitations, the AMS has not published a revised edition. The Beijing World Publishing Corporation accepted to publish this corrected version, but holds distribution rights restricted to Mainland China.

## Scientific Integrity
To ensure the mathematical community has access to the correct definitions and theorems, this PDF is provided here as an **Extended Erratum** to the AMS edition. 

**For all research purposes, this text supersedes the 2013 AMS print.**

## Major Corrections
*   **Groupoid Morphisms:** The definition is corrected to include the necessary injectivity conditions on objects.
