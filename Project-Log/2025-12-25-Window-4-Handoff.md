# Session Overview

This session (Christmas 2025) was a major expansion of the archive. We
moved from establishing workflows to archiving foundational historical
documents and cutting-edge preprints.

# Philosophical Update: \"Cleaning the Dust\"

The user defined a refined philosophy for the project: Diffeology is not
merely an \*extension\* of differential geometry, but a
\*\*high-resolution investigation\*\* of it. The goal is to \"track down
the slightest trace of unusual behavior\" in the corners of the
classical theory---singularities, boundaries, and quotients---that are
usually smoothed over or ignored.
