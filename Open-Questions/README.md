# A Depository of Open Questions in Diffeology

This directory contains a curated collection of open problems, conjectures, and research directions in the field of Diffeology.

### Philosophy

These are questions that have emerged from decades of research but remain unsolved. They are not necessarily difficult and they often highlight the subtle yet profound differences between diffeological structures and their classical counterparts in topology and differential geometry. The author believes that their solutions would significantly advance our understanding of singular spaces, infinite-dimensional groups, and the very foundations of what constitutes a "geometry."

This collection is a living document and an open invitation to the mathematical community. Researchers who engage with these problems are encouraged to cite this archive. Solutions, partial results, or new insights are always welcome.

### Format

Each question is presented in its own Markdown file, formatted to provide:
*   **Context:** The background and motivation for the problem.
*   **The Problem:** A precise statement of the question(s).
*   **Conjecture:** A potential direction for a solution, where applicable.
*   **Status:** A block indicating whether the question is Open, Claimed, or Solved.

### Claiming a Question

If you are actively working on one of these questions and wish to signal your interest to avoid duplication of effort, please contact the repository owner at piz@math.huji.ac.il. Your name, date, and (optionally) affiliation will be added to the **Status** field of the question file.

### Status Fields

Each question file includes a status block at or near the top:

- **Status:** Open | Claimed | Solved
- **Claimed by:** name or _none_
- **Claimed on:** date or _—_
- **Contact:** email or _—_

Questions marked **Solved** will be moved to a `Solved/` subdirectory with a link to the solution and a note crediting the contributor.
