# Open Question 5: Geometric T-Duality via Prequantum Groupoids

> **Date Added:** January 12, 2026
> — **Status:** Open

### Context

T-Duality is a duality in String Theory that relates two different classical geometries which yield identical quantum physics. Mathematically, in the context of **Topological T-Duality**, this is often modeled using principal circle bundles.
*   Let $\pi: X \to M$ be a circle bundle with Chern class $c_1(X) \in H^2(M, \mathbb{Z})$, equipped with a connection/flux form $\omega$.
*   The **T-Dual** is a distinct circle bundle $\pi^\vee: X^\vee \to M$ with Chern class $c_1(X^\vee)$ determined by the fiber-integration of $\omega$.
*   The dual space $X^\vee$ carries a dual flux $\omega^\vee$ determined by the Chern class of the original space $X$.

Essentially, T-Duality exchanges the **curvature of the bundle** (topology) with the **background flux** (geometry).

### The Diffeological Insight

In the construction of the Prequantum Groupoid [1], the **Total Group of Periods** $P_\omega$ is defined as the subgroup of $\mathbb{R}$ generated by two distinct sources:
1.  **Spherical Periods:** Integrals over spheres (capturing the local curvature/flux $\omega$).
2.  **Toric/Surfacic Periods:** Integrals over cycles related to the fundamental group (capturing the global geometry/winding).

Since T-Duality swaps "Flux" (Spherical) with "Geometric" (Toric), it suggests that while the individual components of the period group swap roles, the **Total Group $P_\omega$ itself should remain invariant**.

### The Question

Can T-Duality be formulated as a canonical isomorphism between the prequantum groupoid of a space and the prequantum groupoid of its dual?

$$ \mathbf{T}_ {\omega}(X) \cong \mathbf{T}_{\omega^\vee}(X^\vee) $$

Specifically:
1.  Does the "Total Torus of Periods" $\mathbb{T}_ \omega = \mathbb{R}/P_\omega$ serve as the fundamental T-duality invariant?
2.  Can we prove that the "Quantum System" (the groupoid) is unique, and that the classical spaces $X$ and $X^\vee$ are merely two different "Skeletons" (sections of units) embedded within the same unique Quantum Fog?
